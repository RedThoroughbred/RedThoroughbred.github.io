Support

RedSynth Pad is made by one person. If something's wrong or unclear, he wants to know.

What you need

  • An iPad with iPadOS 18 or later, or a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later.
  • Nothing else. A MIDI keyboard, an Apple Pencil, and GarageBand are optional.

Quick answers

The plugin doesn't show up in GarageBand or another music app

Open the RedSynth Pad app once, then quit the music app and open it again. More in the guide.

The Mac trackpad doesn't play notes

Rest the pointer over the pad, or press ⌘⇧T to hold it there. Esc frees it.

Notes sound late

Bluetooth headphones add a delay. Use the speakers or wired headphones for playing.

I bought it by mistake. Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too: sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com, find RedSynth Pad and request a refund.

Is there a subscription?

No. One purchase covers iPad, Mac and the Audio Unit, with no subscription and no in-app purchases.

Contact

Email redthoroughbreds@gmail.com. You'll hear back from Seth, who makes the app. It helps to include:

  • your iPad or Mac model and its system version, and the app's version (About: the small "i" on iPad, the app menu on a Mac);
  • whether it happened in the app or in a music app, and which one;
  • what you did and what you saw or heard. A screenshot or a short screen recording is great.

Your photos are yours. Don't send one unless you're happy to; a description of the picture is usually enough.
