Privacy

RedSynth Pad does not collect any data.

RedSynth Pad has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no network access. It never sends anything anywhere.

Your photos

Photos you open in RedSynth Pad are used on your device to make a sound and a palette of light. They stay on your device.

On iPad, photos are chosen with the system's photo picker, so the app sees only the photo you pick, never your library.

When you use RedSynth Pad as an Audio Unit inside another app, the photo is saved with that app's project so the project reopens as you left it. It goes wherever that project goes, and nowhere else.

Your sounds, loops and samples

Sounds, loops and samples you make stay in the app's own storage on your device. In version 1.0 the Audio Unit extension doesn't share that storage: inside another app it keeps its sound in that app's project.

The microphone

RedSynth Pad asks to use the microphone only when you choose to record a sample, and your device asks you first. Recordings stay on your device. The Audio Unit extension never uses the microphone.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

This website

This site is static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers of its own. GitHub, as the host, may log technical details such as IP addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement.
