Fixes, questions, words

When something's wrong, start at the top of this page. Most silence has one of five causes.

No sound

  1. Is the device's volume up, and the iPad's mute off? Check Control Centre.
  2. Where is the sound going? Bluetooth headphones that are connected but in their case will swallow it.
  3. Do the keys light up? Look at the strip at the bottom. If keys light but you hear nothing, it's the output (steps 1 and 2). If no keys light, your touches aren't arriving (see below).
  4. Is the sound itself quiet? Pick another from Sounds. Some textures fade in slowly: hold the note.
  5. Is Ladder bass selected, and you're playing high? It's a bass. Play lower.

The Mac trackpad doesn't play

  • The pointer must be over the pad. The Mac sends trackpad touches to what's under the pointer.
  • Press Hold the pointer (⌘⇧T) so it stays there. Esc frees it.
  • The RedSynth Pad window must be the front window. Click it once.
  • If the on-screen keyboard is showing instead of the pad, press ⌘⇧K.

A note won't stop

Press All Notes Off (⌘.), or lift every finger and change the pad's mode. If it's a long reverb or delay tail, it will fade: turn Space down.

Notes sound late

Bluetooth audio is slow. Wireless headphones add a delay you can feel when you play, around a tenth of a second or more. Use the built-in speakers or wired headphones for playing. This is true of every instrument app.

A photo won't load

  • PNG, JPEG and HEIC work. Other formats (RAW files, PDFs, videos, Live Photo motion) don't. Export or screenshot the picture first.

The plugin isn't in my music app

  1. Open the RedSynth Pad app once.
  2. Quit the music app completely and open it again.
  3. GarageBand on a Mac: check Settings → Audio/MIDI → Enable Audio Units.
  4. Another music app: use its own rescan of Audio Units, if it has one.
  5. Still missing: restart the device. Then write to Seth.

My samples or loops aren't in the plugin

In version 1.0 the plugin can't see the samples and loops you make in the app: the two don't share storage yet. Play them in the app. The plugin can't record from the microphone either; that's Apple's rule for all plugins.

It stutters

  • Close other heavy apps.
  • In a music app, raise its buffer size in the audio settings.
  • Strike, Space and big unison sounds do the most work. Fewer notes at once helps.

Questions

Do I need to know music theory?

No. Set the pad to Major pentatonic and every note fits. The Chords mode plays whole chords that belong together. The strip at the bottom tells you what you just played, which is a nice way to learn.

I chose a built-in sound and my photo is gone. Where is it?

It's kept. Open Sounds: it's under Your photos, as you left it. Or tap Back to in the empty photo area. The app keeps your newest 24 photo sounds. See Your photo sounds.

Can I change every knob, not just the four big ones?

Yes. Edit in the header opens every control of the sound, with a search and a line of help for each. See Edit.

I moved the line but the sound didn't change.

The line plays the sound when the photo plays as Scan. Strike and Space read the photo their own way. The Line panel says so and has a Switch to Scan button.

Will two people get the same sound from the same photo?

Yes. The same file always makes the same instrument. A different photo, even of the same scene, makes a different one.

Can I use photos I didn't take?

The app doesn't check. If you publish music or pictures made with someone else's photo, their rights still apply to the photo. Your own photos, drawings and paintings are always fine.

Can I use the sounds in music I release or sell?

Yes. What you play and record is yours.

Does my photo go anywhere?

No. The app has no network access at all. See Privacy.

Is it one purchase for iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Yes, with the same Apple account, and Family Sharing works.

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes. The same app runs on iPhone, laid out for the phone, upright or on its side.

Does it run on Intel Macs?

No. Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later.

Is there a VST or Windows version?

No. RedSynth Pad is an Audio Unit for Apple devices.

How many notes can play at once?

Sixteen for the synth, plus the drums and the samples, which have their own.

Can I record what I play?

Record loops inside the app. To record audio into a song, load RedSynth Pad as a plugin in GarageBand or another host and record there.

I saw a video of painting on the sound with a brush. Where is it?

That's Sculpt: the brush button at the top, once a photo is in. See Sculpt.

How many sounds can one set of photos make?

Two counts, with the maths shown. At least 1028,275 different spaces can be saved from one set of photos; that is a lower bound, and most of them would sound alike. And a rough count of about 7.6 trillion settings at least 5 cents or 3 dB apart: back-of-envelope, not a listening test, and about the same for any photos. Nothing beyond those two is claimed.

Words

Audio Unit, AUv3Apple's kind of plugin. An instrument that loads inside a music app.
BloomHow long a photo's held tone takes to swell in.
BreakdownThe twenty seconds where a new photo is taken apart on screen.
CutoffWhere a filter starts removing brightness. Lower is darker.
CentA hundredth of the step between two neighbouring keys. An ear can tell about 5, for a steady tone, in good conditions.
EditThe panel, or Mac window, with every control of the sound.
EngineHow a photo is played: Scan, Strike or Space. A music app calls them Classic, Struck and Space.
EnvelopeThe shape of a note over time: attack, decay, sustain, release.
FilterThe part of a synth that removes highs or lows. (Photo filters, like Cartoon, are a different thing: they change the picture.)
HostA music app that loads plugins, such as GarageBand.
LFOA slow wave that moves a control for you.
Loop (in a space)The curve of light that reads the held tone out of the block.
Loop (in Loops mode)A few bars of notes that repeat.
MacroOne knob that turns several controls together.
MaterialWhat you paint into a space in Sculpt: Lead, Iron, Wood, Air or Crystal. Each has a weight and a stiffness.
MalletWhat strikes the object or the space at the start of a note.
MIDIThe language keyboards and music apps use to say "this note, this hard".
MPEMIDI where every finger has its own bend and pressure.
ParameterA control a music app can see and record.
RoleUnder Strike, what a photo becomes: a ringing object, a plucked string, a ladder bass or a clean lead. A music app calls them Struck, Pluck, Ladder bass and Clean.
Scan lineThe line across the photo showing the part you hear. It runs down, across or on a slant, and can move by itself (the Line panel).
SculptChanging a photo sound by hand with a brush: painting one photo, or the block of a space.
SignatureA pattern worked out from every pixel of a photo. It sets the instrument's fine details, and the same photo always gives the same one.
SingThe tone that holds under a strike.
StrikeThe hit at the start of a note, and the tones that ring from it.
UnisonSeveral copies of a note at once, slightly apart, for a thicker sound.
VelocityHow hard a note was played.
VowelThe "ee", "ah" or "oo" quality a photo's colour gives its tone.
