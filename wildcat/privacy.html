Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Wildcat Mesh is an iPhone app for your own Meshtastic and MeshCore LoRa radios.

We do not collect any data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it has no servers of ours to send anything to.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your chats, contacts, the radios in My Mesh, block list, gifts and settings are stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Secrets you enter (repeater admin passwords, the Den access token, a remembered hunt channel) are kept in the iPhone Keychain.
  • The Home Screen widget reads a small summary the app writes on the same iPhone.

Your radios and the mesh

  • The app talks to your radios over Bluetooth. Bluetooth stays active in the background only to keep your radios connected.
  • Messages you send are transmitted by your radio over the LoRa mesh. Anyone on a channel can read what is sent on it, and the Public channel is readable by everyone nearby. Private channels are encrypted by the radio firmware with the channel's key. Radios that relay traffic to the internet (MQTT) may carry mesh messages further.
  • If you choose to set your radio's position from the phone, your radio broadcasts that position on the mesh.

Location

Location is used on your iPhone for Fox Hunt, Daily Fox, Territory, Geo Gifts, coverage walks and, if you choose, setting your radio's position. It is not sent to us or anyone else by the app. A Geo Gift is locked to a place; the place itself isn't sent in readable form. Maps are shown with Apple Maps.

The optional Den

If you set up a Den (a base station you run yourself, for example on a Raspberry Pi), the app connects only to the address you enter, on your own network or VPN. Nothing goes to us.

Reports and sharing

  • Report… opens an email draft addressed to the developer with the message you're reporting. Nothing is sent unless you send that email.
  • Hunt invites and gift links leave the app only when you share them (for example with a QR code or the share sheet).
  • Notifications are local to your iPhone. There is no push service.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
