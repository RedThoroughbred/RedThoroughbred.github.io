Wildcat Mesh

One iPhone app for Meshtastic® and MeshCore LoRa radios: chat on both, bridge them together, and play outdoor games like Fox Hunt and Geo Gifts.

Contact

Questions, bugs or feedback: redthoroughbreds@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and which radio and firmware you use.

To report a message or a person you met over the mesh, long-press the message in the app and choose Report…. The app fills in an email to the same address for you to send. You can also Block anyone from the same menu.

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
  • A Meshtastic radio, and/or a MeshCore radio running the Companion (Bluetooth) firmware. Wildcat Mesh can be connected to one of each at the same time.
  • No radio yet? Open Play → Practice. Every screen works with pretend radios that live on the phone, and nothing is transmitted.

Getting started

  1. Close the official Meshtastic or MeshCore app first. A radio talks to one phone app at a time.
  2. Meshtastic: open the Meshtastic tab and tap Find my node. MeshCore: open the MeshCore tab and pick your node. The first connection may ask for the node's PIN.
  3. Your radios appear in My Mesh, with what each one does in plain English.

Common questions

How does the bridge work?

Your phone relays one private Meshtastic channel and one private MeshCore channel to each other while Wildcat is open and both radios are connected. Public channels can't be bridged. Start a message with @name to reach someone privately on the other network.

Why can't I see other people's channels?

A channel is a shared key. Your radio can only read channels you've added; everyone shares Public.

Is the app free?

Yes. There is no account, no subscription and no advertising.
