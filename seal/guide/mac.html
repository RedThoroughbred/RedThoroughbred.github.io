Seal and open on a Mac

Seal speaks OpenPGP, the same as gpg. Files cross between the phone and any computer.

Seal on the Mac, open on the phone

  1. 1

    Get your iPhone’s public key onto the Mac: Vault page, Share public key, AirDrop.

  2. 2

    On the Mac, import it once:

    gpg --import "Alex-s-iPhone.public-key.asc"
  3. 3

    Seal a file for it:

    gpg --encrypt --recipient "Alex's iPhone" notes.md
  4. 4

    AirDrop notes.md.gpg to the phone, open it with Seal, Open with Face ID.

Seal on the phone, open on the Mac

Seal the file for a key the Mac has, such as your Ledger or a backup key, and open it on the Mac:

gpg --output notes.md --decrypt notes.md.gpg

A file sealed only for the iPhone’s key opens only on that iPhone: its private half never leaves the chip.

A file with a passphrase

Anyone with GnuPG 2.3 or newer opens it with:

gpg --output notes.md --decrypt notes.md.gpg

and types the passphrase. Copy how to open it gives these lines with the right file name.
