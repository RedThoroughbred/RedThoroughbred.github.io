Seal, step by step

Nine short guides, from your first key to a locked Claude Code session. Each one is a few steps, with a picture of the screen. The same guides are in the app, under More, Guides, and they work offline.

  1. 01Start hereSeal is the key your computer doesn’t have. Two minutes to your first key.The four pages · Make this iPhone’s key · Pick a look · What Seal never does · These guides, in the app
  2. 02Keep files in a vaultA vault is a named set of keys, like Personal or Business. Seal a file for it, and the vault keeps it.Make a vault · Seal something for it · Find it again · Change or delete a vault
  3. 03Open a sealed fileA file sealed for your key opens with Face ID, or with your Ledger’s PIN.Bring the file in · Open it
  4. 04Swap keys with someoneTo seal a file for someone, you need their public key. To get files from them, they need yours.Send your public key · Add theirs · Put them in a vault · They don’t have Seal?
  5. 05Lock your Claude Code sessionsA locked session takes no message and runs no tool until your phone says yes with Face ID.What you need · Pair your phone (once) · Turn the lock on · Turn the lock off · Stuck? · Lost your phone? · Away from the Mac · What it does and doesn’t do
  6. 06Sign a git commit from your phoneThe key that signs your commits is in your phone, and a commit is signed only after you’ve read it.Set up a repository (once) · Sign a commit · GitHub’s Verified mark · Sign a message you type
  7. 07Use a LedgerAlready have OpenPGP keys on a Ledger? Seal uses them over USB-C, and never changes them.Connect it · Use its keys · Why use both
  8. 08Seal and open on a MacSeal speaks OpenPGP, the same as gpg. Files cross between the phone and any computer.Seal on the Mac, open on the phone · Seal on the phone, open on the Mac · A file with a passphrase
  9. 09QuestionsShort answers to what people ask first.Where are my files? · I ticked two keys. Do I need both to open the file? · What if I lose my iPhone? · What if I set up Face ID again? · Does Seal send anything anywhere? · Does someone need Seal to open what I send? · What can someone see when I’m not looking? · Which hardware keys work?
