Lock your Claude Code sessions

A locked session takes no message and runs no tool until your phone says yes with Face ID.

AI agents now work on your computer for you. Anyone who gets into your computer, or into your account, can talk to them too. The session lock puts your phone in the way: a Claude Code session under the lock holds every message and every tool call until your iPhone signs a pass. A pass lasts 30 minutes, and nothing on the computer can make one, because the key is in your phone.

What you need

  • Seal on your iPhone, with this iPhone’s key made (see Start here).
  • A Mac with Claude Code, and the same Apple Account on both, so the clipboard is shared.
  • The Mac side of the lock, seal-gate. For now it comes with Seal’s source; a one-step install is coming.

Pair your phone (once)

  1. 1

    On the phone: Sign page, then Copy SSH key under This iPhone’s signing key. That is the public half: safe to copy.

  2. 2

    On the Mac, in Terminal:

    seal-gate --setup "$(pbpaste)"

The Mac now trusts that one key, and only it, for passes.

Try one round, with no session

seal-gate pass try

The Mac puts a request on the clipboard and waits three minutes. On the phone:

  1. 1

    Sign page, Paste a signing request.

  2. 2

    Read it: it names your Mac and when the pass ends.

  3. 3

    Sign with Face ID, then Copy the signature.

The Mac sees the signature and says the pass is good.

Start a locked session

claude --settings "$(seal-gate settings)"

Only that session is under the lock; your other sessions are not. Now send it any message:

  1. 1

    Without a pass, the message is held: “Your message was not taken, and nothing runs.” The request is already on the clipboard.

  2. 2

    On the phone: Paste a signing request, read it, Sign with Face ID, Copy the signature.

  3. 3

    Send the message again. It runs, and the pass is good for 30 minutes.

To lock again before the 30 minutes are up:

seal-gate pass drop

Away from the Mac

With Remote Control, the held message shows the request as one seal://sign?… line. Copy the whole message on the phone: Paste a signing request finds the line inside it. Sign, Copy the signature, and paste the signature back as your next message. The lock reads it there.

What it does and doesn’t do

  • It holds messages and tools in sessions started under it. A command typed with ! at the Mac’s own keyboard is not held, but whoever sits at your keyboard can open Terminal anyway. From the phone, a line starting with ! arrives as a plain message, and is held.
  • While a pass is good, the session works as usual, with Claude’s own permission questions.
  • The lock’s files belong to your user account. A hardened version, with its rules in an admin-owned file, is planned.
  • It can’t stop someone using a stolen login on Anthropic’s own servers. It stops them running anything on your computer.
