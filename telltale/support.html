Support

Telltale is made by one person. If something's wrong or unclear, he wants to know.

Start with the guide

The user guide walks through every check, the results, the PDF report and what to do if you find something. Its troubleshooting chapter covers the usual snags.

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 18 or later.
  • A dark room for the light checks: lights off, curtains closed.
  • A classic infrared TV remote for the infrared self-test (optional; many streaming remotes use Bluetooth instead).
  • The rental's Wi-Fi for the network check. Nothing else: no account, no puck, no extra hardware.

Quick answers

The infrared check doesn't see my TV remote

Use a classic infrared remote and hold a button down 10–30 cm from the camera being tested, then try the next camera. Some iPhone cameras filter infrared too strongly to see it at all; if none of yours sees it, rely on the lens glint scan.

The lights-off test won't start

The room has to get much darker than it was. Switch off every light and close the curtains: a bright window or a lamp left on keeps it waiting. Keep the phone propped up and still.

The network check says Local Network access is off

Go to Settings → Telltale and turn on Local Network, then tap Listen again. The check only listens to what devices announce; it never connects to them.

The tracker check doesn't find my AirTag

iOS doesn't let apps hear AirTags. Your iPhone warns you on its own when an unknown AirTag travels with you: check that Settings → Notifications → Tracking Notifications is on.

My results disappeared

Results are kept only while the app is open, and New room clears them. Save a report (PDF) from Results to keep a copy.

It ringed something that isn't a camera

That's expected: shiny screws, glass, water drops and small LEDs can glint or glow too. Every ring is a place to look, never a verdict. Look closely, then tap I looked: not a camera.

I bought it by mistake. Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too: sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com, find Telltale and request a refund.

Is there a subscription or ads?

No. Telltale is free, with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads, ever.

Contact

Email redthoroughbreds@gmail.com. You'll hear back from Seth, who makes the app. It helps to include:

  • your iPhone model, its iOS version, and the app's version;
  • which check you were running;
  • what you did and what you saw. A screenshot helps.

If you think you've found a camera: don't touch it, photograph it where it is, report it to the booking platform or the front desk and to the local police, and leave if you feel unsafe. More in the guide.
