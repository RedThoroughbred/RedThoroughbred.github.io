Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy covers the Telltale iPhone app.

We do not collect any data. Telltale has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no internet connections, and there are no servers of ours to send anything to.

The camera

  • The lens glint scan, the infrared check and the lights-off test use your iPhone's camera and flashlight. Video is analysed on your iPhone as you scan. It is never uploaded or recorded.
  • Each spot Telltale finds keeps a small snapshot of where it was, so you can recognise the place. Snapshots are kept in the app's memory only, and are gone when you close the app or start a new room.

Motion

Telltale uses the iPhone's motion sensors to tell you when you're sweeping too fast to catch a lens reflection, to show which directions you've swept, and to notice if the phone moves during the lights-off test. Motion data stays on your iPhone.

The network check (Local Network)

  • When you start the network check, Telltale listens for about 25 seconds to what devices on the Wi-Fi you joined announce about themselves (their Bonjour names and service types), to point out the ones that describe themselves as cameras.
  • It only listens. It never connects to, logs in to, scans or probes any device or the network.
  • What it heard is kept in the app's memory only, and is gone when you close the app or start a new room.

The tracker check (Bluetooth)

  • When you start the tracker check, Telltale listens for about 75 seconds to Bluetooth advertisements, to point out item trackers (such as Tile, Samsung SmartTag and Find Hub tags) that stay close to you.
  • It only listens, only while the app is open. It never connects to a tracker or any other device, and it doesn't start Bluetooth until you start the check.
  • What it heard is kept in the app's memory only, and is gone when you close the app or start a new room.

The PDF report

If you tap Save a report (PDF), Telltale makes the report on your iPhone and opens the share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send or save it.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Kept only while the app is open: the spots and lights found and their snapshots, what the network and tracker checks heard, and your progress through the room check. Closing the app clears them.
  • Kept between launches, in the app's settings on your iPhone: which places you've ticked off by hand, which of your cameras can see infrared, your light choice and scan settings, and whether you've seen the flashing-light warning.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of it.

The puck

A future version may work with an optional Wi-Fi listener you build yourself with open-source firmware (the puck). This version doesn't. If that changes, this policy will say exactly what the puck does and keeps before the app can use it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
